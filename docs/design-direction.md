# Yonas Bezu — Portfolio Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: The Velvet Programme
Very Brief Intro: A dark, theatrical editorial identity built like a collector’s circus programme: ink, oxblood, brass, and strong typographic pacing. It frames Yonas as a seasoned stage artist rather than a generic entertainer.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Sunlit Transit
Very Brief Intro: A warm, modern travelogue with parchment, faded saffron, and documentary-style image crops that trace an international performance life. The tone is generous, human, and quietly kinetic.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Ring of Motion
Very Brief Intro: A graphic, kinetic art direction using oversized circles, red signal marks, and modular poster fragments to make juggling patterns feel like choreography. It is bold, contemporary, and slightly mischievous.
Probability: 0.02

## Chosen approach: The Velvet Programme

### Design Movement
Contemporary theatre poster design meeting Swiss editorial typography and the shadow-rich atmosphere of a small, intimate circus tent.

### Core Principles
1. **Stage the story:** Every section should feel like a new act in a programme, not a generic web block.
2. **Tension through contrast:** Use deep negative space against brass typography and selective oxblood accents.
3. **Motion with restraint:** Movement should suggest a ball’s arc or a hat’s rotation, never become decorative noise.
4. **Earned authority:** Lead with the 18+ years of international experience and the discipline behind the spectacle.

### Color Philosophy
Ink-black and smoked charcoal create the quiet before the lights come up. Oxblood is the ownable stage color: warm, physical, and unmistakably theatrical. Antique brass is reserved for moments of emphasis, as if the type itself were catching a spotlight. Ivory is used sparingly to keep body copy warm and readable instead of stark.

### Layout Paradigm
Use an asymmetrical poster composition: a slim fixed-feeling programme rail, offset hero typography, wide image fields, and editorial columns that shift their alignment between sections. Avoid a centered stack; the eye should travel like an act across a stage.

### Signature Elements
1. A slim vertical programme rail with act numbers and a small circular mark.
2. Brass hairline rules and arc motifs that imply juggling trajectories.
3. Cropped portrait and performance panels with caption-like labels, as if lifted from a printed playbill.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel physical and deliberate. Buttons respond with a slight press and a brass highlight; navigation anchors glide to sections; performance cards reveal a little more information on hover without hiding essential copy. No interaction should feel like a game or a carousel.

### Animation
Use short, snappy transitions under 300ms for buttons, links, and card reveals. On load, let the rail and headline enter with a subtle upward drift and opacity fade, staggered by 60ms. Add a slow, barely perceptible brass-orbit drift to decorative arc lines. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by disabling non-essential keyframes and keeping only color/focus transitions.

### Typography System
Display: **Cormorant Garamond**, 700/600, with generous line-height and occasional italic emphasis for act names. Body/UI: **Manrope**, 400/500/700, used for readable paragraphs, navigation, labels, and CTAs. Hierarchy: oversized editorial hero title, compact uppercase eyebrow labels with letter spacing, and sentence-case body copy with a 68–72ch measure.

### Brand Essence
**Positioning:** Yonas Bezu is an Ethiopian circus artist for producers, cultural venues, and event teams who want disciplined, high-energy juggling acts with international stage experience.

Personality adjectives: **magnetic, precise, worldly**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are vivid and declarative. CTAs are direct invitations, never hype. Microcopy uses short, grounded phrases that sound like programme notes.

Example lines:
- “Two acts. One unmistakable stage presence.”
- “Book the moment the room starts looking up.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact circular mark made from two offset rings: one ring suggests a bouncing ball’s impact point, the other a hat brim in orbit. Pair it with a custom-feeling stacked wordmark treatment—“YONAS” in spaced small caps above “BEZU” in a large high-contrast serif—rather than a default logo font.

### Signature Brand Color
**Oxblood Stage — `#8D2E2C`**. It should appear in the programme rail, active navigation states, and the hero’s key accent moments; it is never used as a full-page gradient.

## Content constraints

- Use résumé facts only: Yonas Bezu, Ethiopian circus artist, 18+ years of experience, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, international work across Africa, India, Dubai, Egypt, Kenya, Tanzania, and Mozambique, and the listed contact details.
- Show only two acts on the site: **Bouncing Ball** and **Hat Juggling**.
- Do not mention, label, illustrate, or imply the excluded **ladder balance** act anywhere in visible website copy or visual assets.
- Do not invent testimonials, ratings, clients beyond the résumé, or performance statistics.
